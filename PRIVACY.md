# Privacy Policy — Fishy Motion

Fishy Motion is a single-player game. It does not require an account and it does not include analytics, advertising, or a backend.

## Data on the device

- Level stars, unlocked themes, seen moves, and settings are stored in the app’s Application Support folder.

## Data we do not collect

- No name, email, or identifier is sent anywhere.
- No usage analytics.
- No tracking across apps or websites.
